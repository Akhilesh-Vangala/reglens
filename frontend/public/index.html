A member-service copilot for credit-union staff, grounded in the federal consumer-banking regulations.

The model proposes; deterministic code decides. Every claim is a verbatim quote checked against the pinned regulation text, every date and dollar limit comes from a calculator, and anything unsupported goes to a person.
